# A new Era

A new era does not introduce itself gradually, it is BOOMCHACKALACA there and draws your attention at once.
So does this great, surprisingly smooth, anis-tequila mixture.
The addition of oleo saccharum of 19:1 lemon:Grapefruit does the rest.
It is balancing the whole to a maximum:

  * 1 1/2 oz. Tequila
  * 3/4   oz. Arak Touma 50°
  * 1/2   oz. Lime juice
  * 1/2   oz. Oleo saccharum as described above
  * maybe a pinch of salt, but that's it
